Why Elachee

Over Twenty Years of Happy Campers.

We know choosing a camp is a big decision. For over twenty years, North Georgia families have made us part of their summer — and their kids have never looked back.

20+ Years Running Camp
1,440 Acres of Chicopee Woods
1,000s of Satisfied Campers

Our staff members don't just teach about the outdoors. They help children build a real relationship with it. Every trail hike, every creek day, every moment of discovery is designed with one goal: send your child home better than they arrived.

What They'll Gain

This Is What You're Really Signing Them Up For

Memories are great. But this is what parents actually notice when they pick up each day.
Real Confidence

When a child navigates a trail or figures out which plants are edible, they learn something no classroom can teach. They are capable.

Scientific Curiosity

We turn every rock, every stream, every bird into a question worth asking. Kids leave genuinely curious about the world around them.

Genuine Friendships

Something about exploring the woods together forms bonds fast. Many Camp Elachee friendships last well beyond a single summer.

Independence & Resilience

Away from screens and in the middle of something real, children discover they can handle more than anyone expected. Including themselves.

Environmental Stewardship

When children truly understand the natural world, they want to protect it. We plant those seeds early.

Joy of Being Bored (the Best Way)

No algorithm feeding them the next thing. Just open land, open time, and the discovery that outside is more interesting than any screen.

They come home with stories, confidence, and muddy socks — and they can't wait to come back.

2026 Camp Themes

Every Week Is a Different World to Explore

Sessions run Monday–Friday. Many families register for multiple weeks.
Week 1
Wilderness Wanderings

Elementary · K–6th Grade

June 1–5, 2026

Campers dive deep into everything that calls the forest home: trees, animals and habitats. This week also brings knot tying, archery, shelter building and plenty of traditional camp activities.

  • Archery
  • Knot tying
  • Shelter building
  • Forest habitats
Week 2
Aquatic Adventures

Elementary · K–6th Grade

June 8–12, 2026

Get ready for lots of water fun this summer! Campers will explore Walnut Creek and discover all the critters that call the water home, building real knowledge about aquatic habitats.

  • Creek exploration
  • Aquatic critters
  • Water science
Week 3
Winged Wonders

Elementary · K–6th Grade

June 15–19, 2026

From the tiny Bumble Bee to the Great Horned Owl. If it has wings, we're watching it and learning its story. Campers discover how and why these creatures use flight for movement.

  • Bird identification
  • Entomology
  • Field observation
Week 4
Space Bound

Elementary · K–6th Grade

June 22–26, 2026

Blast off to faraway places! Campers take a closer look at Earth's solar system, working to solve mysteries of space and Earth alike through hands-on experiments. 3… 2… 1… launch.

  • Solar system
  • Space experiments
  • Earth science
Week 5
Aquatic Adventures

Elementary · K–6th Grade

June 29–July 3, 2026

Can't get enough of the creek? Campers explore Walnut Creek and all of its aquatic wonders in this popular repeat session. Perfect for families who missed June 8–12, or campers who want more creek science.

  • Creek exploration
  • Aquatic critters
  • Water science
Week 6
Ancient Elachee

Elementary · K–6th Grade

July 6–10, 2026

Travel back in time — before buildings, before roads, before everything. Campers explore the Native peoples who called this land home, dig into dinosaurs and fossils, and uncover rocks hiding stories millions of years old.

  • Native peoples
  • Dinosaurs & fossils
  • Rocks & minerals
Week 7
Camp Rewind

Elementary · K–6th Grade

July 13–17, 2026

The greatest hits are back. Campers relive the most beloved parts of summer. Favourite games. Favourite activities. Favourite moments. All wrapped up in one feel-good week to close out the elementary season.

  • Fan favorites
  • Classic games
  • Best of summer

A Typical Day

Your Child's Day, Hour by Hour

No mystery. No anxiety. We want you to know exactly what's happening while your child is with us. Every day has a shape to it. Here's what that looks like.
8:15 AM

Drop-Off & Morning Arrival

Drop-off begins at 8:15. Campers check in with their group leader and settle into the morning with a light activity.

9:00 AM

Morning Adventure Block

The heart of the day. Trail hikes, habitat exploration, hands-on science, and theme-driven activities led by our naturalist staff.

11:30 AM

Lunch & Free Play

Campers eat the lunch they brought from home and enjoy some unstructured outdoor time. Real rest. Real fun.

12:30 PM

Afternoon Adventure Block

Archery, nature crafts, group games, wildlife encounters — or creek exploration on Tuesdays and Fridays. Every afternoon ties back to the week’s theme.

2:15 PM

Wrap-Up & Debrief

Groups come together to share what they found. Campers gather their gear — usually already talking about tomorrow.

2:45 PM

Pick-Up

Pick-up begins at 2:45. Please plan to arrive on time so every camper gets a smooth, happy send-off.

Every Tuesday & Friday

Creek Days at Chicopee Creek

Every Tuesday and Friday, campers explore Chicopee Creek. It is one of the most loved parts of the whole summer. Water stays ankle-deep in most areas. No swimming ability needed. Just good shoes and a sense of adventure.

  • Bring a towel and closed-toe water shoes
  • Wear creek outfit under clothes.
  • No swimming ability required. All creek time is supervised. Most areas are ankle-deep.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Else You're Wondering

Answers to the questions parents ask most.
Browse by topic
What ages can attend Camp Elachee?

Elementary Camp is open to children who will be at least 5 years old on or before September 1, 2026, and are entering Kindergarten through 6th grade. Specialty Camps are for students entering grades 4–8.

What are the exact camp hours?

Drop-off begins at 8:15 AM and pick-up begins at 2:45 PM, Monday through Friday. If you need to pick up early, it must be by 2:00 PM. Before and after care is not available at this time.

Does my child need prior outdoor experience?

Not at all. Camp Elachee welcomes children at every comfort level. Our naturalists meet every child exactly where they are.

Does my child need to know how to swim for Creek Day?

No swimming ability is required. Water in most areas of Chicopee Creek is no higher than ankle-deep, and all creek activities are fully supervised. Campers should wear closed-toe water shoes and arrive wearing their creek clothes.

When does registration open?

Elachee Family Adventurer Members receive early access beginning February 3, 2026 at 10:00 AM. General public registration opens March 3, 2026 at 10:00 AM. If a week is full, a waitlist option is available.

Are there discounts available?

Yes. Elachee Family Adventurer Members receive 10% off. Discovery Plus Members receive 20%. Families registering multiple children at the same time receive a 5% sibling discount. Contact us for your sibling discount code.

What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Refunds are available until the cancellation deadline. After the deadline, refunds require a doctor's note. Week transfers are permitted as space allows. Requests must be made at least 10 business days before the start of the new camp week.

Can I request that my child be grouped with a friend?

Unfortunately we are not able to accommodate friend group requests. Our groupings are based on age and grade to maintain appropriate staffing structures. Campers who are close in age are often in the same group or participate in joint activities throughout the day.

What should my child bring each day?

A labeled water bottle, lunch in one lunchbox they can carry, at least 2 snacks, and an optional hat. Elachee provides sunscreen and bug spray. Storage is limited, so pack light.

Is Camp Elachee nut-free?

Yes. Camp Elachee is a nut-free environment to protect the safety of all campers. Please do not pack any food items that contain nuts of any kind.

Can you accommodate special needs or medical conditions?

We work hard to make our camp as inclusive as possible. If your child has special needs, a medical condition, or requires specific accommodations, please reach out through our contact page before registering so we can discuss options together.

Will I receive communication during camp week?

Yes. You'll receive a weekly camp newsletter via email on the Monday of your child's camp week. It includes highlights of what's planned and helpful reminders to help you connect with your camper about their experiences.

What if my child is upset or struggling during camp?

Our staff are trained to recognize and respond to children who are having a hard time. We are good at turning a tough start into a great week. If your child is experiencing ongoing difficulty, a staff member will contact you.

Is there a CIT or Junior Counselor program?

We are not hosting a Junior CIT or CIT program for the 2026 season. Older campers who have aged out of elementary programming should check out our Specialty Camps. Rooted in Nature and Expedition Elachee are designed specifically for grades 4–8.

Where is Camp Elachee located?

Camp Elachee takes place at the Elachee Nature Science Center, located at 2125 Elachee Drive, Gainesville, GA 30504, within the 1,440-acre Chicopee Woods Nature Preserve.

What Happens After You Click Register

Five quick steps. Most families finish in about five minutes — then you're booked and we take it from there.

  • 1
    Create your account

    Our registration lives on the Campsite platform. Create a simple family account on your first visit. It takes about two minutes.

  • 2
    Select your week(s) and camper(s)

    Browse available themes and dates. Love more than one theme? Add multiple weeks. If a session is full, join the waitlist. Spots open up regularly.

  • 3
    Complete your camper profile

    Enter your child's age, grade, any relevant medical information and emergency contacts.

  • 4
    Pay securely online

    Processed through Campsite's secure checkout. You will receive an immediate confirmation email.

  • 5
    Watch for your welcome email
