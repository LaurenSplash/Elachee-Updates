Finding the right activity for your kids shouldn't feel like homework. When looking for places to take kids, you want something that gets them off screens, sparks genuine curiosity, and doesn't drain your wallet, ideally all three at once.

Georgia families are lucky! The state is packed with outdoor preserves, hands-on science centers, and wildlife experiences that deliver on all counts. And if you're in or near Gainesville, one destination stands above the rest: Elachee Nature Science Center.

Fun things to do with kids this weekend

Weekend plans don't have to be elaborate to be memorable. Some of the best experiences for kids are the simplest: a trail to hike, a creek to explore, a bridge to cross.

At Elachee, families have access to one of the largest protected forests in Northeast Georgia, the 1,440-acre Chicopee Woods Nature Preserve. With nearly 12 miles of trails winding through forests, wetlands, and streams, kids can spot wildlife, cross suspension bridges, and discover what's living under every rock and log.

What families love on a weekend visit

  • Short nature hikes through wooded terrain (with wildlife sightings!)
  • Seasonal family events and festivals throughout the year
  • Indoor exhibits featuring live animals and hands-on displays
  • Guided nature programs for kids of all ages

Educational places to take kids in Georgia

Families across North Georgia choose Elachee because it combines outdoor adventure with hands-on science learning. What sets nature-based learning apart is how it sticks. Kids who learn about ecosystems while walking through one retain far more than what they'd pick up in a classroom.

Elachee's programs and exhibits cover:

  • Native plants and animals of Georgia
  • Forest ecosystems and how habitats work together
  • Conservation and environmental stewardship
  • Space science and exploration

Indoor exhibits at Elachee

Elachee's campus features three indoor exhibits that keep kids engaged on rainy days or anytime curious minds need a new challenge.

Space Encounters

Look up! This exhibit introduces kids to the universe beyond our atmosphere, including planets, stars, galaxies, and the science of space exploration.

Woodland Wonders

Georgia's forests are full of life, most of which goes unnoticed by the average passerby. Woodland Wonders pulls back the curtain on native wildlife, plants, insects, and soil ecosystems.

The Cocoon

Designed especially for toddlers and their caregivers, The Cocoon offers a quiet, calming space where families can pause and recharge during their visit.

Outdoor places: active fun in nature

The best family adventures usually involve some dirt. Outdoor exploration gives kids the kind of full-body, full-brain engagement that's hard to replicate indoors.

  • Hiking nearly 12 miles of forested trails
  • Exploring creek beds and wetland habitats
  • Birdwatching and wildlife observation
  • Visiting the nature center exhibits mid-hike

Ask any child what they remember about a field trip, and they'll tell you about the thing they touched.

Hands-on activities kids love

Hands-on learning isn't just more fun — it's more effective. When kids interact directly with their environment, curiosity becomes the engine of education.

  • Animal encounters and wildlife education programs
  • Nature scavenger hunts on the trails
  • Guided hikes with naturalist educators
  • Creek exploration and habitat observation

Affordable places to take kids

Great family experiences don't require a big budget. In fact, some of the most memorable outings cost almost nothing — and nature is usually at the top of that list.

Plan your visit to Elachee

If you're searching for the best places to take kids near Gainesville, Elachee is a clear standout. From your child spotting their first salamander or finally understanding what a life cycle means, every visit offers something new to discover.
