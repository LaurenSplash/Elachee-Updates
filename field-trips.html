Quick Answer

What Is an Elachee Field Trip?

An Elachee field trip is a naturalist-led outdoor science experience for Pre-K–12 students. Depending on the program, students get hands-on with real habitats and ecosystems through activities connected to classroom learning goals.

  • Forest Habitats
  • Creek Ecosystems
  • Aquatic Life
  • Geology
  • Astronomy
  • Wildlife
  • Environmental Stewardship
The Teacher Reality

Planning a meaningful field trip shouldn't feel like one more lesson plan to write

Teachers already have enough to manage: standards, testing schedules, transportation, permission slips, budgets, and keeping students engaged. A field trip should support your curriculum, not add confusion to your plate.
Hard to Justify the Day Away

You need more than "a fun day outside." You need a trip that connects clearly to learning goals and classroom standards.

Hard to Coordinate the Logistics

Transportation, timing, chaperones, weather, and student needs can make planning feel overwhelming before the day even arrives.

Hard to Know Which Program Fits

With different grade levels, topics, and seasonal options, it's tough to figure out which experience is right for your students.

Elachee helps you turn the field trip from one more thing to coordinate into a learning experience that supports your standards, your students, and your school calendar.

Your Outdoor Education Partner

Elachee makes outdoor science simple, standards-minded, and hands-on.

We know teachers have to think about more than whether a field trip sounds fun. You need standards alignment, clear logistics, age-appropriate instruction, safety-minded planning, and a program your administrators can support.

Elachee field trips are built to reinforce what you're already teaching — with guided labs, hikes, and hands-on science set inside the 1,440-acre Chicopee Woods Nature Preserve. Pre-K through 12th-grade programs are designed to support Georgia Standards of Excellence, and we offer onsite, in-classroom, seasonal, and Lake Lanier aquatic options so you can pick the format that fits your class, calendar, and curriculum goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Else You're Wondering

Answers to the questions teachers and administrators ask most.
Browse by topic
What grade levels can attend an Elachee field trip?

Elachee offers field trip experiences for Pre-K through 12th grade, with programs designed to fit different developmental levels, curriculum goals, and science topics.

Are programs designed to support Georgia Standards of Excellence?

Yes. Elachee field trips are designed to support science learning and the Georgia Standards of Excellence by giving students real-world context for concepts they are studying in class.

Can Elachee come to our school?

Yes. Elachee in Your Classroom brings hands-on environmental education to schools within a 60-mile radius.

What if we're not sure which program is right for our grade level?

Start by submitting an inquiry. Elachee can help match your grade level, learning goals, timing, and logistics with the best available option.

Are field trips available year-round?

Many programs are available year-round, while some seasonal offerings are limited to specific months. Spring and fall are often high-demand seasons, so early planning is recommended.

What happens if weather affects our field trip?

Because many programs include outdoor components, weather may affect timing or activities. Elachee will communicate with your group about weather-related adjustments.

How do we request a field trip?

Use the "Request a Field Trip" button to submit your school, grade level, group size, preferred dates, and program interests. An Elachee team member will follow up to confirm availability and recommend the best program fit.

How much does an Elachee field trip cost?

Pricing varies by program type, group size, and format. Submit a field trip request and Elachee will help confirm the best option and current pricing for your group.

How long is a typical field trip?

Program length varies by experience. Some programs are designed for shorter classroom-style visits, while onsite and aquatic programs may require more time. Contact Elachee for current program timing.

How far in advance should we request a field trip?

Spring and fall are high-demand seasons, so early requests are recommended. The sooner you reach out, the easier it is to match your group with preferred dates.

What should students wear?

Students should wear weather-appropriate clothing and closed-toe shoes suitable for trails, outdoor exploration, and hands-on learning.
